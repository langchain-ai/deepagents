- [Subagents and Skills](subagents-skills.md) - Deep Agents separates synchronous and remote delegation across explicit state and lifecycle boundaries. - [Talon Channel Admission](talon-channel-admission.md) - How Talon channel adapters admit messages and reactions, bind trusted conversation identity to host-owned turns, and constrain cancellation, authorization, and delivery. - [Talon Scheduling and Background Work](talon-scheduling.md) - Talon persists origin-scoped cron jobs, claims them before unattended execution, and routes non-silent results to their recorded origin. This page covers schedule semantics, storage and recovery, delivery, pinned skills, and the distinct background-subagent behavior of chat and cron turns.
- [Tools and Filesystem](tools-filesystem.md) - FilesystemMiddleware exposes backend-backed file tools while separately applying model-surface filtering, filesystem policy, human approval, and content lifecycle controls. Composite routing remaps file paths across backends, whereas execution remains attached to the composite default backend.
